Hi! I'm Nicole Banda

I am a front-end developer based just 15 minutes out of Portland, OR. I originally studied anthropology, psychology, and Japanese at Willamette University, but took a Python course in my last year of college and found my love for coding. It was perhaps my love of languages and puzzles that steered me towards this field. I took various workshops specializing in front-end development and enjoyed developing many projects. Using both my studies in social science and my ever-growing coding skills, I am eager to work on products that help communities with social issues or that assist with people's daily lives.
